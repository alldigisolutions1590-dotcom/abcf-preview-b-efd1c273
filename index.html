How we deliver

01

Same-day in Long Beach

Same-day delivery in Long Beach, and next-day on most of what we make. We also deliver to the towns around the city — ask us when you order.

02

A photo before it goes

We send you a photograph of the finished arrangement before it is delivered. It is the thing our customers write to us about most.

03

Hand-delivered by us

Our own drivers carry every order — to homes, hospitals, offices and venues. No courier network, no parcel van.

The shop · Bixby Knolls

Eighty years,
one family.

“Harvest the beauty of the season.” — the shop's autumn campaign, abeautifulcalflorist.com

A Beautiful California Florist is family-owned and operated, and minority woman-owned. It has been part of Long Beach for more than eighty years, with 203 designs in the catalogue online — and every one of them leaves the shop in a hand.

  1. 01

    Choose — or call

    Pick a piece from the catalogue, or tell us your colours and the flowers you love and we will build it. Call the shop: (562) 437-4106.

  2. 02

    We arrange it

    Made to order the morning it leaves the shop, from flowers cut for that day's work — and photographed before it goes out.

  3. 03

    Our driver delivers

    Hand-delivered across Long Beach — same day where the day allows, next day on the rest.
